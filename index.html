About Tertiary SA

Tertiary SA is a free, registration-free platform designed to help students explore higher education opportunities in South Africa.

Our Mission

To provide a comprehensive, easy-to-use directory of all South African universities and colleges, along with detailed course information, admission requirements, and funding opportunities.

What We Offer
  • 29 Universities: All public universities in South Africa, including universities of technology.
  • 1,490 Colleges: A complete list of registered colleges across all 9 provinces.
  • Course Details: APS scores, subject requirements, application dates, and closing dates for each course.
  • Bursary Information: Comprehensive funding opportunities with direct links to official applications.
  • Student Services: Information on academic support, counseling, accommodation, and career services.
How It Works

Simply browse or search for institutions, click on courses to see detailed requirements, and use the direct links to visit official institution websites. No registration required.

Data Sources

All information is compiled from official institution websites, the Department of Higher Education, and publicly available sources. We strive to keep information accurate but always verify with the official source.
